Calculation Math Explanations

1. Excel Sheet Math Mode (Strict Parity)

In your uploaded Excel file (Salary Calculater.xlsx), the Special Allowance is calculated using the formula:
=B24 - SUM(B18:B19, B21:B23)
where both Employee PF (B21) and Employer PF (B22) along with Gratuity (B23) are deducted from Total CTC to calculate Special Allowance.

2. Standard Corporate Payroll Mode

In standard corporate Indian payroll accounting, CTC represents the total employer liability:
CTC = Gross Salary + Employer Retirals (Employer PF + Gratuity)
Employee PF is deducted from Gross Salary rather than from CTC. Both options are supported seamlessly so you can match both your Excel file and statutory HR requirements!
